About BHC

Digitizing nature, connecting the world.

Responsible research use

Cite the record. Credit the image. Protect the locality.

Every published specimen states its preferred citation and reuse terms. Photograph credit and licence are recorded per image where supplied. Exact locality data may be generalized or withheld to protect vulnerable species, private land and collecting sites.
